Client and server

26.3 is not 1.21.11. Mojang dropped the old 1.x numbers at the end of 2025 — 1.21.11 was the last release to use them — and moved to a year-and-drop scheme, so 26.3 is the third drop of 2026. The two are different versions and this mod runs on the new one: it asks for ~26.3 and will refuse to load on 1.21.11. To check which you are on, look at the bottom-left corner of the title screen.

The mod

Thirty-five switches, each one per dimension, so the haunting can be tuned to a single effect or switched off entirely. Every default is the behaviour the mod already had, so an existing world carries on exactly as you left it.

Seven apparitions

The watcher on the ridge, the runner across the water, the thing at your window: each with its own behaviour, and none of them guaranteed to stay where you saw it.

Non-destructive

Tunnels are dug, groves are stripped and pyramids appear, but your builds are never touched. Every change is one he makes, not one he does to you.

Iron is a ward

Everything hammered out of iron keeps him back — armour per piece, a sword or stick in either hand, and iron lying on the ground. A full set is a strong ward, not a wall.

Thirteen sounds, all original

Whispers, footsteps, a pickaxe where no one is mining, a bell for the cross, ten minutes of his music and two and a half of hers. Every sound is generated for this mod; nothing is taken from anywhere else.

Alex walks with you

Summon her the way you summon Steve. She follows, she fights off what comes at you, and while she is beside you and he is not, her own theme plays — a bell over a warm bed, in his key, and nothing like his.

The fog closes in

As he gets closer the fog thickens. With a shader pack loaded it falls back to a vignette no pack can throw away, so the effect survives whatever you render with.

He holds a grudge

Killing animals and the dark below ground push him closer through four tiers; carrying iron pushes him back. Opt-in, so a world you are already playing carries on as it is.

His eyes cast light

Install LambDynamicLights and he lights the ground around him. The mod ships the definition; nothing else is needed.

Music

Two pieces, both generated for the mod. His is ten minutes of drone, wind, distant bells and something moving under the floor, with a real arc and silent ends so it loops. Hers is two and a half: a bell over a warm bed, in his key, for the companion who walks beside you. Neither is fetched until you ask for it.

World seed

Type a word instead of a number and Minecraft turns it into one: the number below is what the game makes of his name. Every structure the mod builds is placed by hand rather than by worldgen, so it behaves the same on any seed you choose — this is just the one with his name on it.

10528182

Type Herobrine into the seed box and the game arrives at the same world: Java Edition hashes the text into a signed 32-bit number, and that hash is 10528182. Copy the number if you would rather paste than spell.
