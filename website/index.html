He was never in the game. Now he is.

A Fabric mod for Minecraft: Java Edition. Staged hauntings, seven apparitions and an original score for him and for her. Nothing of yours is ever destroyed.

26.3 is not 1.21.11. Mojang dropped the old 1.x numbers at the end of 2025 — 1.21.11 was the last release to use them — and moved to a year-and-drop scheme, so 26.3 is the third drop of 2026. They are different versions and the jars are not interchangeable, so there is one of each: herobrine-3.0.5.jar for 26.3 and herobrine-3.0.5+1.21.11.jar for 1.21.11. Take the one that matches your game. To check which you are on, look at the bottom-left corner of the title screen.

The 1.21.11 jar is server-side only. The hauntings, the sounds, the saved settings and the /herobrine command all work, but the HUD fog overlay, his glowing eyes, the in-game config screen and the Mod Menu entry are not in it: they are written against 26.3's rendering code, which does not exist in 1.21.11. Everything is in the 26.3 jar.

The mod

Thirty-five switches, each set per dimension, so you can tune the haunting to a single effect or turn it off. Every default matches what the mod already did, so an existing world carries on as you left it.

Non-destructive

Tunnels are dug, groves are stripped and pyramids appear, but your builds are never touched. Every change is one he makes, not one he does to you.

Seven apparitions

The watcher on the ridge, the runner across the water, the thing at your window. Each behaves differently, and none is guaranteed to stay where you saw it.

Iron is a ward

Iron armour (per piece), an iron sword or stick in either hand, and iron on the ground all keep him back. A full set is a strong ward, not a wall.

The fog closes in

The nearer he is, the thicker the fog. With a shader pack loaded it falls back to a vignette that no pack can remove.

He holds a grudge

Killing animals and spending time in the dark underground push him closer through four tiers. Carrying iron pushes him back. It is opt-in, so existing worlds stay as they are.

Alex walks with you

Summon her the way you summon Steve. She follows and fights what comes at you. When she is beside you and he is not, her own theme plays: a bell over a warm bed, in his key.

Thirteen sounds, all original

Whispers, footsteps, a pickaxe where no one is mining, a bell for the cross, ten minutes of his music and two and a half of hers. All generated for this mod.

Music

Three tracks, all generated for this mod. Each loops without a seam, and none is fetched until you ask for it. The third is the one the in-game Herobrine music disc plays.

A seed to start on

Type a word instead of a number and Minecraft turns it into one: the number below is what the game makes of his name. Every structure the mod builds is placed by hand, not by worldgen, so it works on any seed — this one just has his name on it.

10528182

Type Herobrine into the seed box and the game arrives at the same world: Java Edition hashes the text into a signed 32-bit number, and that hash is 10528182. Copy the number if you would rather paste than spell. The hashing is a rule of Java itself rather than of any one Minecraft version, so the number is the same on 26.3 and on 1.21.11.
