## What's new

- **Alex**: a companion, and Steve's counterpart. Summon her with `/herobrine alex spawn`; she
  follows, she fights off what comes at you, and you can set her to stay or follow exactly as you
  can with him. She has her own switch, off by default like the other new ones, so a world you are
  already playing carries on as it was.
- **Her theme**: two and a half minutes, generated for this mod the way his is — a bell over a
  warm bed, in his key, and looping without a seam. It plays while she is walking with you and he
  is nowhere near. Two tracks at once is noise, and a warm one underneath a haunting is worse than
  silence.
- **He holds a grudge**: an opt-in escalation system. Killing animals and lingering in the dark
  below ground push a dread score up through four tiers, and each one brings him closer and more
  often. Carrying iron pushes it back down. Off by default, so the haunting escalates on time
  alone unless you turn it on. `/herobrine hauntstate` prints the score.
- **A rebuilt haunting track**: his ten minutes now have real reverb, proper harmonics and room to
  breathe, instead of the flat drone it was.
- **Hostile Herobrine gives up**: from the moment anything hurt him he used to be permanent — no
  expiry, never despawned, and not even counted against the cap on new sightings. He now vanishes
  after three minutes with nobody in sight. Being hurt still turns him hostile, and he still hunts
  for as long as you are there.
- **A settings screen that fits**: descriptions on the Haunting and Audio & World tabs were being
  cut in half.
- **Lighter on memory**: everything the mod holds per player is now released when they leave.
  Four of the seven maps it keeps were never cleared, so they grew for as long as the server ran.
- **A showcase website**, at the same address, with both tracks to listen to and a seed to start
  on.

## Installation

Use **herobrine-3.0.4.jar** with Minecraft **26.3**, Fabric Loader **0.19.0 or newer**, Fabric API
**0.165.0 or newer**, and Java **25 or newer**. Remove older Herobrine JARs from the mods folder
before installing. Mod Menu is optional. Server commands require cheats in singleplayer or operator
permission on a server.

Two notes on versions. **26.3 is not 1.21.11**: Mojang dropped the old 1.x numbers at the end of
2025 and 26.3 is the third drop of 2026, so this mod will refuse to load on 1.21.11. And Java moved
with it — 26.3 needs Java 25, where 1.21.11 ran on Java 21.

Alex and the grudge are both off by default. Turn them on in the settings screen, per dimension.

## Optional shader test

**optimism-26.3-test.zip** is a separate, unofficial experimental shader pack based on Optimism
Shader by Nachtwind1 (MIT), not part of the mod. Put it in the `shaderpacks` folder, not the `mods`
folder, and select it with Iris.
